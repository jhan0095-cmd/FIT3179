Explore where extreme weather events occur, how patterns change over time, and the impacts they have.

View Australian States GeoJSON

4. Natural Earth Country Boundaries
Geographic boundary data used to provide country-level map context.
View Natural Earth data

Data Processing

The event records are processed and organised according to the fields required for each visualisation. Depending on the dataset, these may include event dates, locations, categories and impact measures.

Geographic coordinates and boundary data are used to display event locations and support spatial comparisons. Aggregation is applied where necessary to summarise records by year, state or event category.

Visualisation Approach

Different visualisation idioms are used to explore the distribution and characteristics of extreme weather events across Australia.

Maps highlight spatial patterns, while temporal charts show changes over time. Comparison charts and relationship plots help examine differences between event categories, locations and recorded impacts.
